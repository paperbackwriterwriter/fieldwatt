Privacy policy

How FieldWatt handles information.

Information you provide

FieldWatt receives the information you enter in newsletter, alert, employer, training-program, and candidate-profile forms. This can include your email address and the details you choose to send.

Candidate directory

A candidate profile is private unless the candidate explicitly opts in to the employer-searchable directory. Employers may see only the approved profile fields: name, city/state, work history, certifications, experience, travel preference, and selected work categories. Resume files, email addresses, and phone numbers are never shown in the directory. Candidates can withdraw from the directory or request deletion from the candidate-profile page; either action removes the profile from employer search immediately.

How it is used

We use contact information to deliver requested alerts, confirm inquiries, respond to employer and program requests, and operate the service. FieldWatt does not handle an applicant's employer application; applications are completed with the employer or source site.

Service providers and links

FieldWatt uses service providers to host the site and process notifications. Job links may take you to third-party employer or source sites, which have their own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can use the unsubscribe option included with email communications. For a privacy question or request, use the employer or training inquiry form and include “privacy” in your message.
