About FieldWatt

FieldWatt is the job feed for people who build, install, repair, and maintain renewable-energy infrastructure. It brings wind, solar, storage, and grid field openings together so tradespeople do not have to search dozens of employer sites.

Listings are organized by role, location, and pay where it is listed. The public feed is clearly labeled while FieldWatt connects permitted live sources. Job seekers use the feed and alerts for free; employers and training programs fund the service through clearly labeled placements.
